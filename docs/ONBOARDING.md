# HYROX Onboarding

Status: approved by product owner 2026-10-15. Target: 3–4 minutes.

| # | Screen | Questions | Required | What it changes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your race | Find your race in our HYROX race list (search by city or name), then pick your race day; or "My race isn't listed" and type the date (D-019) · Division (Women Open / Women Pro / Men Open / Men Pro) · Goal: just finish, or a target time | Date, division | Plan length and phases; station loads |
| 2 | Your experience | Level (First-timer → Competitive, one-line description each) · Past HYROX result: finish time and date, optional run and station splits, link to look it up on the HYROX results site (name search turns on once HYROX grants data access, D-019) · Where's your training right now? (Building up / Training consistently / Race-ready; a HYROX in the last 8 weeks pre-selects Race-ready) | Level, training status | Session mix, hard days, simulations; where the plan starts (D-018) |
| 3 | Your running | Typical weekly running · Recent 5K / 10K / half time and date | Weekly running | Starting volume; personal paces (effort-based if skipped) |
| 4 | Your schedule | Training days · Day for the longest session · Minutes available weekdays / weekends · Advanced only: "Do you want double days?" | Training days | Session placement; session length caps |
| 5 | Your equipment | Where you train: Home / CrossFit / Commercial / HYROX club (any combination) → pre-ticked checklist to correct | Yes | Station or substitute used in each block |
| 6 | Limitations | Movements to avoid (jumping, running downhill, deep knee bends, …) · Optional note | No | Removes those exercises |
| 7 | Review | Summary; "When do you want to start?" (Today / Tomorrow / Next Monday, D-033); consent | Start day required at first onboarding | Not asked again once the start day has passed |

Wearable connections are not part of onboarding; athletes add them from settings once integrations exist.

## Rules
- **Division, not sex.** We ask which division the athlete races; that's all the station loads need.
- **Limitations, not diagnoses.** We ask only which movements to avoid, and say why. No injury details.
- **Units.** Miles by default for athlete-facing running distances, with a setting to switch to kilometres. HYROX race segments keep their official 1 km label (shown as "1 km (0.62 mi)" in miles mode).
- **Target finish time** is recorded but does not change the plan yet. It will drive pacing guidance later, once the engine can check it against the athlete's benchmarks.
- Optional questions can be skipped; skipped answers are never guessed silently (e.g. no benchmark → effort-based targets, labelled as such).
