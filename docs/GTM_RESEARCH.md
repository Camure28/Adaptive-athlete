# Go-to-market research

Built step by step with Cameron; each step waits for his feedback. Prices and figures were checked on 2026-10-16 and change often.

## Step 1 — Market and competitors (2026-10-16)

### Market
- HYROX had roughly 1.3–1.5 million participants in the 2025/26 season and expects more than 2 million in 2026/27, over 107 race weekends (The Star, July 2026; Athletech News, July 2026).
- About 14,000 affiliated gyms worldwide (HYROX Chief Growth Officer, April 2026).
- Salt Lake City's first HYROX (Sept 2026) drew about 16,000 athletes, so Utah is a live local market.
- Returning athletes were only 17–29% of each season's field (Hyranking, March 2026): most athletes are first-timers, and many don't come back.

### Subscription benchmarks (Health & Fitness)
- Trial-to-paid about 35% median (Adapty 2026); 39.9% median, 68.3% top decile (RevenueCat 2025).
- First-renewal retention about 30%, the lowest of any category (Adapty 2026).
- Annual plans bring about 60% of revenue (Adapty 2026).

### What it means for Adaptive Athlete (Claude's read, for discussion)
1. The market is real and growing fast, but crowded, and "adaptive" is no longer unique: Repz claims it with an official badge.
2. Our edge has to be proof, not claims: a visible "why your plan changed" review, coach-written rules, safety rules never paywalled.
3. Gyms are the underserved channel: affiliate programming costs gyms $70–179/month and doesn't adapt to each member. Cameron is a coach inside that world.
4. Most racers are first-timers and churn after race day, so pricing should fit a race block and win them back for the next race.
5. Working prices (~$14.99/mo, ~$99/yr) sit below Repz and Runna; fine for launch.
